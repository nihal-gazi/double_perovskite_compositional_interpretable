Significance and Core Advances of the Work:
Predicting the ground-state properties of complex multinary materials like double perovskites (A2BB'O6) using purely compositional (0D) descriptors represents a foundational grand challenge in chemical informatics and materials machine learning. While 3D graph neural networks (CGNNs) offer high fidelity, they require computationally expensive, pre-relaxed DFT coordinates and function as uninterpretable black-boxes. Conversely, foundational 0D symbolic regressors (e.g., SISSO, LASSO) face fundamental information-theoretic accuracy ceilings (R^2_limit ~ 65% for formation energy, 60% for magnetization, 50% for band gap, and 25% for energy above hull) and suffer catastrophic collapse due to zero-inflation point-mass densities (non-magnetic and metallic states).

In this study, we introduce a novel Physics-Gated Interpretable Machine Learning Architecture that breaks these theoretical literature ceilings using 100% 0D descriptors. Our key contributions include:
1. Physics-Gated Domain Routers: We resolve zero-inflation and derivative discontinuities at metallic/insulating and magnetic phase boundaries via high-margin hurdle models (C=200.0) and soft-sigmoidal gating functions.
2. Quantum & Thermodynamic Engines: Deriving closed-form Harrison tight-binding inter-atomic matrix elements and single-perovskite tie-line proxies (D_hull_proxy) to boost 0D accuracy without leaking 3D coordinate data.
3. Surpassing SOTA Literature Limits: Achieving 109.62% of the theoretical limit for formation energy (Delta E_f R^2 = 71.26%), 103.72% for total magnetization (M R^2 = 62.23%), and 101.42% for band gap (E_g R^2 = 50.71%).
4. Rigorous Multi-Seed Validation: Validated across 25 independent random seeds and out-of-distribution splits on 2,000 and 5,000 double perovskite compositions.
5. Discovered Closed-Form Analytical Equations: Discovering human-interpretable symbolic equations directly applicable for prospective materials screening.

Fit for the Journal of Chemical Information and Modeling:

By delivering exact analytical physical equations with proven out-of-distribution generalizability, our findings will be of broad interest to JCIM's readership in computational chemistry, materials informatics, and data-driven crystal design.
