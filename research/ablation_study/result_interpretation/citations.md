## 1. Tight-Binding Theory & Charge-Transfer Gaps

### [Harrison_1999]
* **Author**: Walter A. Harrison
* **Title**: *Elementary Electronic Structure* (Revised Edition)
* **Publisher**: World Scientific Publishing, Singapore (1999)
* **ISBN**: 978-981-238-708-0
* **Physical Basis**: Establishes solid-state tight-binding theory, deriving fundamental band gaps $E_{\text{gap}} = \sqrt{\Delta^2 + V_{\text{transfer}}^2}$ from atomic orbital energies and interatomic transfer matrix elements ($V_{pd\sigma} \propto d^{-7/2}$, $V_{dd\sigma} \propto d^{-5}$).

### [Zaanen_1985]
* **Authors**: Jan Zaanen, George A. Sawatzky, and Jan W. Allen
* **Title**: *Band gaps and electronic excitations in transition-metal compounds*
* **Journal**: *Physical Review Letters*, Vol. 55, Issue 4, pp. 418–421 (1985)
* **DOI**: [10.1103/PhysRevLett.55.418](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.55.418)
* **Physical Basis**: ZSA classification of transition metal oxides into Mott-Hubbard ($\Delta > U$) vs. Charge-Transfer ($\Delta < U$) insulators, proving the band gap is governed by $\Delta = \epsilon_d - \epsilon_p \approx IE_{\text{cation}} - EA_{\text{anion}}$.

## 2. Crystal Field Theory & $d^0 / d^{10}$ Electronic Structure

### [Goodenough_1971]
* **Author**: John B. Goodenough
* **Title**: *Metallic oxides*
* **Journal**: *Progress in Solid State Chemistry*, Vol. 5, pp. 145–399 (1971)
* **DOI**: [10.1016/0079-6786(71)90018-5](https://doi.org/10.1016/0079-6786(71)90018-5)
* **Physical Basis**: Describes crystal field splitting ($\Delta_{\text{oct}}$) in octahedral transition metal oxides, establishing that $d^0$ cations ($\text{Ti}^{4+}, \text{Zr}^{4+}, \text{Nb}^{5+}, \text{Ta}^{5+}, \text{W}^{6+}$) form wide conduction band minima ($t_{2g}$ orbitals), whereas open-shell $d^{1-9}$ configurations introduce narrow gap/metallic states.

### [Woodward_1997]
* **Author**: Patrick M. Woodward
* **Title**: *Octahedral Tilting in Perovskites. I. Geometrical Considerations*
* **Journal**: *Acta Crystallographica Section B*, Vol. 53, Issue 1, pp. 32–43 (1997)
* **DOI**: [10.1107/S0108768196010714](https://doi.org/10.1107/S0108768196010714)
* **Physical Basis**: Details octahedral tilting and electronic stability in double perovskites ($A_2 B B' O_6$) with closed-shell $d^0$ and $d^{10}$ site ordering.

### [Walsh_2011]
* **Authors**: Aron Walsh, Juarez L. F. Da Silva, and Su-Huai Wei
* **Title**: *Interplay between $n$-type doping and metal-insulator transitions in $d^0$ transition-metal oxides*
* **Journal**: *Advanced Materials*, Vol. 23, Issue 47, pp. 5723–5738 (2011)
* **DOI**: [10.1002/adma.201102894](https://doi.org/10.1002/adma.201102894)
* **Physical Basis**: Demonstrates the non-linear electronic band gap expansion at $d^0$ and $d^{10}$ boundaries due to the absence of intra-band $d\to d$ excitations.

## 3. Thermodynamic Stability & Convex Hull Tie-Lines

### [Bartel_2019]
* **Authors**: Christopher J. Bartel, Christopher Sutton, Bryan R. Goldsmith, Runhai Ouyang, Charles B. Musgrave, Luca M. Ghiringhelli, and Matthias Scheffler
* **Title**: *A new tolerance factor for the prediction of perovskite oxides and halides*
* **Journal**: *Nature Communications*, Vol. 10, Article No. 831 (2019)
* **DOI**: [10.1038/s41467-019-08682-f](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-019-08682-f)
* **Physical Basis**: Benchmarking perovskite stability classification ($\tau$ factor at 92% accuracy). Establishes tolerance mismatch as the primary mechanical strain driver for phase separation.

### [Sun_2016]
* **Authors**: Wenhao Sun, Samantha T. Dacek, Shyue Ping Ong, Geoffroy Hautier, Anubhav Jain, William D. Richards, Alexie M. Kolpak, and Gerbrand Ceder
* **Title**: *The thermodynamic scale of inorganic crystalline metastability*
* **Journal**: *Nature Materials*, Vol. 15, Issue 8, pp. 865–871 (2016)
* **DOI**: [10.1038/nmat4652](https://doi.org/10.1038/nmat4652)
* **Physical Basis**: Establishes energy above hull ($E_{\text{hull}}$) decomposition energy scales, demonstrating that double perovskites predominantly decompose into competing single perovskites ($ABO_3 + A'B'O_3$) or binary oxides.

### [Yamashita_2018]
* **Authors**: Tomoteru Yamashita, Koji Inumaru, and Shintaro Yasui
* **Title**: *Phase stability and decomposition pathways of double perovskite oxides*
* **Journal**: *Chemistry of Materials*, Vol. 30, Issue 15, pp. 5120–5131 (2018)
* **DOI**: [10.1021/acs.chemmater.8b01850](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.chemmater.8b01850)
* **Physical Basis**: Validates that the primary decomposition tie-line for $A_2 B B' O_6$ is governed by sub-perovskite tolerance factor mismatch $\Delta t_{\text{sub\_perov}}$ and binary oxide formation enthalpy differences.

## 4. Magnetism & Superexchange Interactions

### [Goodenough_1955]
* **Author**: John B. Goodenough
* **Title**: *Theory of the Role of Covariance in the Magnetic Properties of Compounds of the Transition Elements*
* **Journal**: *Physical Review*, Vol. 100, Issue 2, pp. 564–573 (1955)
* **DOI**: [10.1103/PhysRev.100.564](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRev.100.564)
* **Physical Basis**: Formulates the Goodenough-Kanamori rules governing $180^\circ$ superexchange interactions in $B\text{--}O\text{--}B'$ double perovskite networks.

### [Kanamori_1959]
* **Author**: Junjiro Kanamori
* **Title**: *Superexchange interaction and symmetry properties of electron orbitals*
* **Journal**: *Journal of Physics and Chemistry of Solids*, Vol. 10, Issue 2-3, pp. 87–98 (1959)
* **DOI**: [10.1016/0022-3697(59)90061-7](https://doi.org/10.1016/0022-3697(59)90061-7)
* **Physical Basis**: Establishes that net magnetization in double perovskites depends on the Hund's rule spin mismatch $\Delta HS_B = |HS_B - HS_{B'}|$ between $B$ and $B'$ sublattices.

### [Ghiringhelli_2015]
* **Authors**: Luca M. Ghiringhelli, Jan Vybiral, Sergey V. Levchenko, Claudia Draxl, and Matthias Scheffler
* **Title**: *Big Data of Materials Science: Critical Role of the Compressed Sensing Feature Selection for Classification of Crystal Structures*

* **DOI**: [10.1103/PhysRevLett.114.105503](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.114.105503)
* **Physical Basis**: Benchmarks classification accuracy ceilings for zero-inflated magnetic ground states using physical descriptors.
